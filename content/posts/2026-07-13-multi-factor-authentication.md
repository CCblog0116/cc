## 📖 Article

### Why Passwords Alone Are No Longer Enough

For most of internet history, a **password** (口令) was the only thing standing between an attacker and your account. But passwords have a fundamental problem: they can be stolen, guessed, or reused.

Attackers today use **credential stuffing** (凭证填充) — taking usernames and passwords leaked from one breach and trying them against thousands of other sites. They also use **password spraying** (密码喷洒), trying a small list of common passwords against many accounts. And, of course, they use **phishing** (网络钓鱼) to trick users into typing their passwords into fake login pages.

> "Passwords are the weakest link in most authentication systems." 密码是大多数认证系统中最薄弱的环节。

This is why security teams have moved toward **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)** (多因素认证) — also called **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** (双因素认证) when only two factors are used.

---

### What Is MFA?

MFA requires a user to prove their identity using **two or more factors** from different categories:

| Factor | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Something you know** | Information only the user should know | Password, PIN |
| **Something you have** | A physical object the user possesses | Phone, hardware token, smart card |
| **Something you are** | A biological characteristic | Fingerprint, face, iris |

> **Key idea:** The factors must be independent. If your password is on your phone, and your phone is also your second factor, that is not true MFA. 多因素之间必须独立，否则只是“伪 MFA”。

MFA is now one of the most effective controls against **account takeover (ATO)** (账户接管). According to Microsoft, MFA can block **99.9% of automated attacks** on accounts.

---

### Common Types of MFA

Not all MFA is equally strong. Here is a quick overview:

| Type | How It Works | Security Level | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SMS / Voice OTP** (短信/语音一次性密码) | A one-time code sent via text or call | Lower | Code from your bank |
| **TOTP / HOTP** (基于时间/事件的一次性密码) | Code generated by an app or hardware token | Medium | Google Authenticator, YubiKey OTP |
| **Push Notification** (推送通知) | Phone app asks you to approve a login | Medium | Microsoft Authenticator prompt |
| **Hardware Token** (硬件令牌) | Physical device generates or stores keys | High | YubiKey, RSA SecurID |
| **FIDO2 / WebAuthn** (快速身份在线认证 / 网页认证) | Public-key cryptography, phishing-resistant | Highest | Touch ID, Windows Hello, security key |
| **Biometric** (生物特征) | Fingerprint, face, or voice match | High | Apple Face ID, Windows Hello |

### Why SMS MFA Is Not Recommended

Many people think SMS is "good enough." It is not. Security experts, including **NIST** (美国国家标准与技术研究院), have warned against using SMS as an authentication factor for years.

The main risks are:

- **SIM swap** (SIM 卡交换): An attacker convinces your mobile carrier to move your number to a new SIM card they control. Then they receive all your SMS codes.
- **SS7 attacks** (SS7 信令攻击): Vulnerabilities in the global phone signaling system can let attackers intercept text messages.
- **Social engineering** (社会工程学): Support staff can be tricked into redirecting SMS codes.

> **NIST guidance:** Use **authenticator apps** (认证器应用) or **hardware-backed cryptographic keys** (硬件加密密钥) instead of SMS whenever possible. NIST SP 800-63B 已将 SMS OTP 列为“受限”使用方式。

---

### MFA Fatigue and Push Bombing

Even **push notification MFA** has a weakness: humans. Attackers discovered that if they send a flood of push notifications, users eventually click **Approve** just to make the noise stop. This is called **MFA fatigue** (MFA 疲劳) or **push bombing** (推送轰炸).

In the 2022 **Uber breach** (Uber 数据泄露事件), the attacker reportedly used this technique to bypass MFA and gain access to internal systems. This proves that MFA alone is not enough — the *type* of MFA matters.

> **Better design:** MFA prompts should include context (location, device, app) and require users to enter a number from the screen instead of a simple "Approve / Deny" tap. 增加上下文和数字匹配，可降低 MFA 疲劳攻击成功率。

---

### Phishing-Resistant MFA: FIDO2 and WebAuthn

The strongest form of MFA today is **phishing-resistant MFA** (防钓鱼 MFA). The leading standard is **FIDO2** (Fast Identity Online 2), which includes **WebAuthn** (Web Authentication API / 网页认证 API) and **CTAP** (Client to Authenticator Protocol / 客户端到认证器协议).

How it works:

1. During registration, the authenticator creates a **public-private key pair** (公钥-私钥对) for that specific website.
2. The **private key** (私钥) never leaves the authenticator.
3. During login, the website sends a challenge, and the authenticator signs it with the private key.
4. The browser verifies that the request came from the real website — not a fake phishing domain.

> **Why it resists phishing:** FIDO2 keys are bound to the real domain. If you visit a fake login page, the authenticator will not recognize the domain and will not sign the challenge. 即使进入钓鱼页面，密钥也不会被触发，因为域名绑定机制。

Major platforms now support FIDO2: Windows Hello, Apple Touch ID / Face ID, Android biometric keys, and physical security keys like **YubiKey** (YubiKey 安全密钥).

---

### MFA in the Enterprise: The Identity Perimeter

In enterprise environments, MFA is no longer optional for high-risk accounts. It is the front line of the **identity perimeter** (身份边界) — the boundary between trusted users and the rest of the internet.

Modern identity platforms implement **risk-based MFA** (基于风险的多因素认证), where the system asks for a second factor only when the login looks unusual. Signals include:

- **Impossible travel** (不可能旅行): Logging in from two distant countries in a short time.
- **Unknown device** (未知设备): A new browser or operating system.
- **Anonymous IP** (匿名 IP): Traffic from a VPN, Tor node, or proxy.
- **Atypical location or time** (异常位置或时间): Login outside normal working hours.

> **Modern MFA is adaptive.** It does not challenge the user every time; it challenges when risk is higher. 现代 MFA 强调自适应，在安全与体验之间找平衡。

## 📌 甲方安全工程师视角 (Security Engineer's Perspective)

1. **MFA 覆盖率比类型更重要** — 先让所有用户账号都启用 MFA，再逐步升级到 FIDO2/防钓鱼 MFA。
2. **SMS OTP 不是长期方案** — 高管、域名管理员、财务账号等高风险角色应优先迁移到硬件密钥或平台内嵌 FIDO2。
3. **MFA 疲劳攻击真实有效** — 需要在日志中监控异常登录审批次数，并培训用户拒绝来源不明的推送。
4. **身份平台是新的边界** — 保护 IdP（如 Azure AD / Okta / 企微 SSO）就是保护整个公司的访问入口。
